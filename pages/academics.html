From Nursery to Class 12 — One Continuous Journey

A complete academic path from the very first day of school to the year your child walks into a college entrance exam. Taught by experienced teachers in both English and Hindi medium, following the NCERT-based Uttarakhand Board (UBSE) curriculum — with Science and Arts streams available in Class 11 and 12.

The Academic Journey

Every Stage. Every Year. Every Child Looked After.

From play-based learning in the nursery years to serious board preparation in Class 12, our curriculum grows with your child.

Pre-Primary

Nursery — KG

Play-based learning with big-screen TVs, supervised by specialised female teachers. A gentle, joyful introduction to school.

Primary Wing

Class 1 — 5

Strong foundations in reading, writing, arithmetic, and general awareness. NCERT-based, concept-first teaching.

Middle Wing

Class 6 — 8

Deeper subject learning across Science, Maths, Social Studies, Hindi, English, Sanskrit, and Computer.

Secondary Wing

Class 9 — 10

Serious board preparation with dedicated after-school extra classes for Class 10 students — by experienced professional teachers.

Senior Secondary

Class 11 — 12

Choose Science (PCM or PCB) or Arts. Intensive board preparation with after-school extra classes for Class 12 students.

Senior Secondary — Class 11 & 12

Choose the Stream That Fits Your Future

In Class 11 and 12, every KBIC student picks a stream that matches their goals. Whether your child wants to become an engineer, a doctor, a teacher, a civil servant, or something else entirely — we have the right path, taught by experienced board-exam teachers.

Science · PCM

Physics, Chemistry & Maths

The engineering and technology track. For students who love solving problems and thinking in numbers.

Core Subjects

  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Mathematics
  • English
  • Hindi
Career paths: Engineering (JEE), NDA, architecture, computer science, data science, defence services, B.Sc. programs, polytechnic.
Science · PCB

Physics, Chemistry & Biology

The medical and life-sciences track. For students who want to heal, research, or work in the life sciences.

Core Subjects

  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • English
  • Hindi
Career paths: MBBS (NEET), BDS, B.Sc. Nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy, veterinary, agriculture, biotechnology, life-sciences research.
Arts

Arts & Humanities

The humanities and civil services track. For students who love to read, write, think about society, and shape ideas.

Core Subjects

  • History
  • Geography / Political Science
  • Economics / Sociology
  • Hindi & English
  • Optional (Sanskrit / Home Science)
Career paths: UPSC & state civil services, law (CLAT), B.A., journalism, teaching, psychology, social work, public administration.

How We Teach

The KBIC Teaching Approach

Curriculum alone doesn't build results. It is the way a school teaches — day after day, student by student — that makes the difference.

Bilingual Medium

Every class is taught in both English and Hindi medium. Your child is never left behind because of a language barrier — they grow into both languages at their own pace.

Experienced Teachers

Highly qualified, trained, and experienced professional teachers across every subject — not rotating part-timers. Your child learns from faculty who know them by name.

Board Exam Extra Classes

Free after-school extra classes for Class 10 and Class 12 students by our most experienced teachers. Parents don't need to pay for outside tuition — the school is built to handle board prep itself.
