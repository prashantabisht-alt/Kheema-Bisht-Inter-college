About Kheema Bisht Inter College

Kheema Bisht Inter College — 25+ years of quality education in Khatima, Uttarakhand.

Our Story

Kheema Bisht Inter College was established in the year 2000 in Gaujariya, Khatima, Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand.

Affiliated to the Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE), the college has grown from a small institution into a trusted name in education.

Named after Shri Kheema Bisht, the college carries forward a legacy of service to education and community upliftment.
