How It Works

Admission Process

A simple, transparent 4-step journey from inquiry to confirmed seat — no long queues, no guesswork.

1

Inquire

Fill the form below or WhatsApp our admissions team. We respond within 24 hours on school days.

2

Visit Campus

Walk in any working day between 9 AM and 1 PM — no appointment needed. Meet teachers, see classrooms, ask every question you have.

3

Submit Documents

Student's birth certificate and Aadhar card, Aadhar card of both parents, and 2 passport-size photographs of the student. If your child is coming from another school, please also bring the previous report card.

4

Confirm Seat

Collect the welcome kit and your child is officially part of the KBIC family. Any questions on timings, transport, or fees are answered by our office team in person.
