Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions parents ask before joining Kheema Bisht Inter College.

What classes does Kheema Bisht Inter College offer?
Kheema Bisht Inter College offers complete schooling from Nursery through Class XII. At the senior secondary level (Class XI–XII), both Science and Arts streams are available under the Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE).
Which board is Kheema Bisht Inter College affiliated to?
The college is affiliated to the Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE), Government of Uttarakhand.
When do admissions for the next session open?
Admissions for the 2026-27 session are currently open. Parents can submit an enquiry through the Admissions page or visit the school office on any working day between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM. Saturday campus tours are also available.
What documents are required for admission?
For new admissions, please bring: a recent passport-size photograph, the student's birth certificate or Aadhaar card, parents' Aadhaar card, the previous school's Transfer Certificate (if applicable), and the last report card for any class above Nursery.
Is there an entrance test for admission?
For Nursery to Class I, admission is based on a short interaction with the child and parents. For Class II onwards, a basic written assessment in core subjects is conducted to understand the student's current level and place them appropriately.
What are the school timings?
Regular school hours are 9:00 AM to 2:30 PM, Monday through Saturday. Pre-primary classes finish slightly earlier. Timings may vary in winter and during examinations — please check the latest notice on the News page.
Does the school provide transport facility?
Limited transport support is currently available for nearby villages around Gaujhariya and Khatima. Routes and availability change each session — please contact the school office at 9761621360 for the latest route map and seat availability.
How can I pay fees?
Fees can currently be paid in cash or by cheque at the school office during working hours. An online fee payment option is being set up — see the Fees page for the latest update.
What is the school uniform?
The school uniform consists of a navy blue and white combination, with the school badge on the shirt pocket. Sports uniform and winter wear details are shared at the time of admission. Uniforms are available from designated local vendors.
