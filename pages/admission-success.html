Inquiry Received — Welcome to the KBIC Family

Thank you for reaching out. Our admissions office has your details and will personally contact you within 24 hours on school days.

1

We call or WhatsApp you

Within 24 hours on school days, our admissions team will reach out on the number you shared to answer your questions and suggest the best time to visit.

2

Visit the campus

Walk in any working day between 9 AM and 1 PM. Meet the teachers, see the classrooms, and get every question answered in person — no appointment needed.

3

Bring a few documents

Student's birth certificate and Aadhar card, Aadhar card of both parents, and 2 passport-size photos of the student. If transferring from another school, bring the previous report card too.

4

Collect the welcome kit

Once the seat is confirmed, your child officially joins the KBIC family. Our office team will walk you through timings, transport, and everything else in person.
