Our Teachers & Staff

The heart of Kheema Bisht Inter College is our dedicated team of teachers and support staff. They work every day to help students from Nursery to Class XII grow in knowledge, character, and confidence.

Teaching Faculty

Our faculty includes experienced teachers for Hindi, English, Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Social Science, Sanskrit, Computer Science, and the Arts stream subjects. Each teacher combines strong subject expertise with patience and care for every student.

Male Teaching Staff

Experienced male faculty members teaching Science, Mathematics, Social Science, and senior-wing subjects across Classes VI–XII.

Female Teaching Staff

Dedicated women teachers guiding primary and middle-wing students, as well as language and arts subjects in the senior wing.

Subject Specialists

Teachers specialising in UBSE board-level Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics, preparing students for Class X and XII board examinations.

Primary & Nursery Wing

Warm and patient teachers for our youngest learners, focusing on foundational literacy, numeracy, and social skills in a safe, caring environment.

Our Commitment

Every member of our staff — teaching and non-teaching — shares the same goal: creating a disciplined yet joyful learning environment where students feel supported, challenged, and inspired. Parents are always welcome to meet teachers during school hours to discuss their child's progress.

For admissions or to meet a specific department head, please contact us or visit the campus.
