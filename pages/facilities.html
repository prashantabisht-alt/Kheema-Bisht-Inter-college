Inside the Campus

Everything a Modern School Should Have — and Then Some

From hi-tech smart classrooms to a working science lab, from a trampoline for the little ones to a full sports room for the older students — here is what your child gets access to, every single day.

Hi-Tech Smart Classrooms

Every classroom is equipped with modern projectors and TV screens so lessons come alive, not just from a textbook.

  • Big-screen TVs in Nursery & KG for joyful learning and activity time
  • Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms with decorative learning walls
  • Always-on fans and lights — zero disruption during summer

100% Solar Powered

The entire school runs on solar energy — classrooms, labs, fans, lights, WiFi. Power cuts simply don't interrupt learning at KBIC.

  • Panels installed across every building on campus
  • 24/7 electricity in every classroom and lab
  • No summer discomfort, no interrupted exams, no paused classes

Full Science Labs

Not a showcase — a real, working science lab where students actually do experiments with proper apparatus and chemicals.

  • All working apparatus present and in use
  • Full chemical stock for practical classes
  • Hands-on learning from junior grades upward

Computer Lab + 24/7 WiFi

A fully-equipped computer lab gives students direct exposure to technology skills they'll need for the world ahead.

  • Dedicated computer lab with student workstations
  • 24/7 campus WiFi for research and digital learning
  • Basic-to-advanced computer curriculum

Big Sports Ground

A spacious, properly-maintained playground supports every major sport your child wants to play.

  • Cricket, volleyball, basketball, kho-kho, kabaddi, badminton
  • Sports room fully stocked with equipment for every game
  • Physical fitness as a daily, not occasional, part of school

Play Zone for Little Ones

Unique

For our nursery, KG and junior students, a dedicated, supervised play area full of joyful equipment — led by specialised female teachers.

  • Swings, see-saws and slides for everyday play
  • Indoor trampoline for safe, supervised activity
  • Professional dance classes for those who love to move

Dedicated Care Staff

For Little Ones

We know parents of nursery and KG children worry about the small things — toilet help, a spilled lunch, a tied shoelace. We've staffed the campus to handle every one of them.

  • A special female care assistant dedicated to helping small children with toilet and personal care
  • More than 7 cleaning staff keeping classrooms, toilets and common areas spotless through the day
  • Fresh, hygienic environment your child comes home from happy every day

Getting to School

A Transport Fleet Built Around Student Safety

11 Vehicles. Trained Staff. Cameras Onboard.

Every single KBIC bus and van is driven by a professional, experienced driver and paired with a female conductor who looks after the children from pickup to drop-off. Every vehicle has onboard cameras, and routes cover neighbourhoods across the town and nearby areas.

We know the drive to and from school is something parents think about every day. That's why we designed the transport system the way we did: so you don't have to worry about any of it.
