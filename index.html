What Sets Us Apart

Why Choose Us

Focused academics, strong co-curriculars, and a supportive learning environment.

Hi-Tech Smart Classrooms

Every classroom equipped with projectors, TV screens, proper ventilation and 24/7 lights and fans — thanks to full solar power, learning never stops for a power cut.

Nursery to Class 12 — All Streams

A complete path from nursery to senior secondary, with Science (PCM & PCB) and Arts streams in Class 11 & 12, plus free after-school coaching for Class 10 and 12 board students.

Safety First, Always

100+ CCTV cameras with audio on campus, 11 vehicles with female conductors and onboard cameras, secure boundary wall, RO drinking water, and fire safety across every building.
